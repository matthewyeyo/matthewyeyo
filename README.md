### Hey, I'm Matthew 👋

Self-taught full-stack & AI developer, and an incoming International Undergraduate Program (IUP) IT student at **Universitas Gadjah Mada**. I like building things end-to-end — LLM-powered apps one week, ESP32 hardware the next — and I'm working toward founding **Metis Corp**, an AI company focused on digital literacy, child online safety, and SME tech adoption in Indonesia.
